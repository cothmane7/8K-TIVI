Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information ISTREAM4k collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website and Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

  • Order details — the plan you choose, price, and payment method you select at checkout.
  • Contact details — your email address and, optionally, your WhatsApp number, so we can confirm and activate your order.
  • Messages — the content of any emails or WhatsApp messages you send us.
  • Basic technical data — standard information your browser sends (e.g. device/browser type), used to keep the site working.

We do not take card payments on this website, so we do not store card numbers — payment is finalised with you directly over WhatsApp.

2. How we use your information

  • To process, confirm, and activate your order.
  • To contact you about your order and provide customer support.
  • To improve and secure our Service.
  • To meet legal or regulatory obligations.

3. WhatsApp & email

When you contact us or follow up on an order via WhatsApp or email, your message and contact details are processed through those services in order to communicate with you. Their own privacy terms also apply to messages handled on their platforms.

4. Cookies & local storage

We use minimal browser storage to remember your language preference and to carry your order details between the checkout and confirmation pages. We do not use this to track you across other websites.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with service providers that help us operate (for example, our email/hosting providers) and where required by law.

6. Data retention

We keep order and contact information for as long as needed to provide the Service, support you, and meet legal requirements, then delete or anonymise it.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy? Email us at our support email or reach us on WhatsApp via the Support page.
